\section{Sum-free subgraphs under a density condition}

For a graph $G$ on $n$ vertices, condition (4.2) means that every vertex has
degree at least $\delta n$, where $0 < \delta < 1/2$ is fixed.

\begin{question}
Does every graph $G$ satisfying condition (4.2) contain an induced sum-free
subgraph?
\end{question}
